## Long post

State of the trenches, Sep 17 – Oct 13 🧵

I've been tracking launchpad fees, narratives and launches every day. Here's what four weeks looks like.

1/ Fees are down hard.
Daily fees across 14 non-overlapping launchpad venues peaked at $7.79M on Sep 21 and were $4.03M on Oct 12: −48% from the peak. The weekly average went from $6.86M/day to $4.13M/day (−40%).

2/ It wasn't even. Comparing the first week (Sep 17–23) with the last (Oct 6–12):
• pump.fun $1.61M → $2.21M a day (+37%)
• Pons $2.71M → $531K (−80%)
• LaunchLab −82%, Genius.fun −99%, Flap −13%
pump.fun is now the biggest slice of what's left.

3/ Narratives move faster than ever.
I've tracked 17 narratives in 16 days. 11 are already retired; the median retired one lasted 3 days. The #1 narrative by 24h pool volume changed hands 7 times in 12 snapshots (Super Intelligence, Agency, post-quantum and others trading the top spot).

4/ Attention cooled.
Combined 24h volume of the narratives I track: $141.6M on Oct 5 → $27.3M on Oct 13. (The tracked set changes daily, so read it as the tracked set's attention, not the whole market.)

5/ Launches slowed too.
Indexed token creations: 88.9K per 24h on Oct 2 → 57.4K on Oct 12 (−35%), then a bounce to 70.2K on Oct 13. Completions hit a low of 3,449 on Oct 9.

6/ What's new this week: coins pitching AI credits for holders and creator fees routed to named accounts both showed up in the tracked set on Oct 12–13. Those are the projects' own claims; I'm not verifying payouts or usage.

Caveats: narratives are a curated tracked sample, volumes are pool volume (some coins trade far above their market cap and may include wash trading), and launch counts are beta provider-indexed observations. Fees: DefiLlama. Narratives and launches: Codex. Both via Frames.

Not financial advice.
