How stock token multipliers work

View reward drops

On Robinhood Chain each stock token keeps a fixed raw balance. When the underlying company runs a corporate action, like a stock split or a cash dividend paid by the issuer on the real stock, the token updates an onchain shares per token multiplier instead of minting or burning tokens. The Chainlink oracle quotes the full token price already, so USD marks reflect the multiplier.

Stonk Interns

8,888 on Robinhood ChainStonk Interns is the companion collection to StonkBrokers: 8,888 interns on Robinhood Chain, two for every one of the 4,444 brokers. Intern #N is broker #N's Sigma class V1 intern and intern #N + 4,444 its Divergent class V2 intern, each mirroring the broker's own traits in its class's art style. 56 of them are paired one of ones. Every intern is an ERC-721C token with its own ERC-6551 wallet, art and metadata rendered fully onchain, and traits (job title, pay share, parent status, activation tier) that update themselves as the intern's standing changes.

Two interns for every broker, Sigma and Divergent class, minted out of the broker's own wallet and paid out of the same Clock In rails.

StonkBrokers, the senior desks

A StonkBroker is a full seat on the World Wide Stonk Exchange. Every activated broker elects the tokenized stocks and assets it wants, protocol revenue is swapped into those assets each Clock In round, and the rewards land in the broker's own token bound wallet. Brokers own the floor: they hold the stock, they cast the elections, and now they hire.

Stonk Interns, the pipeline

An intern is the desk next to a broker: lower cost, tied to one real broker, paid a base salary out of that broker's rounds, and climbing a career ladder of onchain job titles. Interns are the easy way into the ecosystem for new traders, for StockFi and DeFi power players in training, and for builders. They use the same Clock In elections, token bound wallets, Smart LP vaults and Safety Deposit Box tools as brokers, at a fraction of the price.

Where this goes

Interns operate the names marketplace for Robinhood Chain and the V2 lending desk for brokers and interns, and they earn fees from both. With the Intern Exchange and Intern Clock In beside them, brokers become the senior desks of a two tier floor and interns the next generation learning to run it.
