## What Is Anonymous Cat (ZCAT)?

Anonymous Cat (ZCAT) is a meme coin on the Solana blockchain that pays its holders in Zcash (ZEC).

The token was created through StonkFun, a Solana launchpad that lets a new token be priced against an asset other than SOL or a stablecoin.

ZCAT launched as a reward coin quoted against ZEC, so its primary liquidity pool is a ZCAT/ZEC market rather than a ZCAT/SOL one.

Under that model, the mint carries a transfer tax that is withheld on every transfer and later distributed to holders in the asset the token is paired against.

For ZCAT, the rate is 3% and the payout asset is ZEC.

The contract address is HcRLc9VDgjLeK154xDawfb1dmVJ98DoSqcwTHGqiDeJR, and it should be verified on [Solscan](https://solscan.io/token/HcRLc9VDgjLeK154xDawfb1dmVJ98DoSqcwTHGqiDeJR) before any on-chain purchase.

## The Story Behind Anonymous Cat (ZCAT)

Anonymous Cat has no founder story in the conventional sense.

The project describes itself in a single line on its liquidity pool profile: the cat stays anonymous, there are no socials, and the rewards are paid in Zcash.

Its identity rests entirely on the Zcash privacy narrative, rendered as a cat concealing its face under a paper bag.

[Zcash is a privacy-focused cryptocurrency](https://z.cash/learn/), and the bag is the visual joke about hidden identity.

Instead of promoting a roadmap or a team, the project ties its proposition to one mechanic: holding ZCAT produces ZEC.

[That framing is why ZCAT is usually described as a yield-style meme coin](https://www.odaily.news/en/newsflash) rather than a utility token.

### Why Anonymous Cat Has No Team, Website, or Whitepaper

The token's public profile states that it has no socials, and no website or whitepaper has been published alongside it.

For readers, this changes where research has to happen.

There is no documentation to read, so the verifiable facts sit in the mint account, the liquidity pool, and the launchpad that created them.

Anyone evaluating ZCAT should treat on-chain data and the launchpad's published mechanics as the primary sources.

## Anonymous Cat (ZCAT) vs Zcash (ZEC): Two Different Assets

ZCAT and ZEC are separate assets on separate blockchains.

[Zcash is an independent privacy-focused cryptocurrency running its own network](https://z.cash/learn/how-to-use-zcash/), while Anonymous Cat is a token on Solana.

ZCAT is not issued by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Zcash, and holding ZCAT gives no claim on the Zcash protocol.

The connection between them is economic rather than technical: ZCAT is quoted against ZEC in its liquidity pool, and its transfer tax is distributed to holders in ZEC.

That connection also gives ZCAT holders indirect exposure to ZEC price movements through the value of the rewards they receive.

### What Is the Difference Between Anonymous Cat and ZCAT?

Anonymous Cat is the name of the project and ZCAT is its ticker symbol.

Unlike a Layer 1 project, where the network and its native coin are distinct systems, Anonymous Cat has no protocol beyond the token itself.

The mint, the liquidity pool, and the reward mechanism are the entire product.

## How Does ZCAT Work?

ZCAT works through three connected parts: the launchpad that created it, the transfer tax written into the mint, and the pairing that decides which asset holders are paid in.

None of them require the holder to take any action.

There is no staking contract, no claim button, and no lock-up period.

The sections below break down each part.

### What Is StonkFun and How Do Reward Coins Work?

StonkFun is a Solana launchpad that lets a creator price a new token against something other than SOL or a stablecoin.

[Its published quote options include tokenized stocks, pre-IPO stocks, currencies, leveraged tokens, SOL, and custom mints](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/developers).

StonkFun launches run on Raydium's liquidity infrastructure, and the ZCAT/ZEC market itself is a Raydium concentrated-liquidity pool.

The platform offers two launch modes, and [reward coins are the mode that carries a transfer tax paid directly to holders](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/developers) instead of a creator fee position.

### How the 3% Transfer Tax Pays Holders in ZEC

ZCAT uses [the Token-2022 standard](https://solana.com/developers/guides/token-extensions/transfer-fee), which allows a transfer fee to be written directly into the mint.

StonkFun's documentation states that the rate is chosen at launch and set permanently from that point.

Every transfer withholds 3% on any venue, and the launchpad holds the authority to collect what has been withheld.

Collected fees accumulate until the pot is worth distributing, then get [split across holders pro-rata and paid out in the quote asset](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/rewards), which for ZCAT is ZEC.

### Why the ZCAT/ZEC Pairing Changes the Trade

Because rewards are paid in ZEC, a ZCAT position carries exposure to two assets at once.

The token's own price determines the value of the holding, while the ZEC price determines the value of the rewards.

Trading volume determines how much is distributed, since the tax only produces income when tokens actually move.

A quiet market produces little reward, no matter how long the position is held.

## Key Features of Anonymous Cat (ZCAT)

- **Rewards paid in a separate asset.** ZCAT holders receive ZEC rather than more ZCAT, so distributions do not add supply pressure to the token itself.
- **No claiming required.** Payouts are pushed out on the launchpad's reward cycle, with no staking, locking, or manual claim step.
- **A tax rate set at launch.** StonkFun states that the 1% or 3% rate is chosen at launch and not changed afterwards.
- **No creator fee position.** Reward-mode launches pay no share of trading fees to the creator, so the tax flows to holders instead of being split with the launch wallet.
- **Token-2022 architecture.** The tax is a property of the mint rather than a rule enforced by one venue, which is why it applies to every transfer, including deposits and withdrawals.

## ZCAT Tokenomics: Supply and the 3% Transfer Tax

ZCAT has no published allocation table, no vesting schedule, and no emission plan.

That is worth stating plainly rather than filling with estimates, because a launchpad token issued without a team does not have the documents a conventional project would publish.

What is verifiable sits on-chain and in the launchpad's own rules.

The mint was created through the launchpad's standard supply template and the live figure sits just under one billion tokens as of September 2026, [which is best checked on the block explorer rather than read from any article](https://solscan.io/token/HcRLc9VDgjLeK154xDawfb1dmVJ98DoSqcwTHGqiDeJR).

The 3% transfer tax is the one figure the launchpad publishes as fixed.

Distribution of that tax is proportional to holdings at the time of each payout, with no tiers, minimums, or lock-ups applied.

### What the 3% Tax Means When You Deposit or Withdraw ZCAT

Because the tax lives in the mint, it applies to your own transfers, not just to trades.

MEXC has published the exact effect on deposits.

3% of each on-chain ZCAT transaction is deducted under the token's own mechanics, and MEXC charges an additional 3% to cover the cost of integrating the token.

The amount credited to your MEXC account is therefore 94% of the amount you sent.

The MEXC announcement describes this deduction in the context of on-chain deposits, and users should check the announcement for the deposit and withdrawal terms that apply.

## Functions of the ZCAT Token

ZCAT serves a narrower set of functions than a utility token, and being clear about that is part of understanding it:

- **Reward accrual** — holding the token in a wallet produces ZEC distributions funded by the transfer tax.
- **Paired exposure** — the token's primary market is quoted in ZEC, tying it to a second asset.
- **Trading and speculation** — ZCAT trades on the MEXC spot market against USDT.
- **Category participation** — ZCAT is one of the launches in StonkFun's reward-coin category, which distributes fees in paired assets.

There is no governance vote, no staking contract, and no fee discount attached to the token.

## The Future of Anonymous Cat (ZCAT)

There is no roadmap to summarise, so the honest version of this section is what the design has already locked in.

StonkFun states the 3% rate was set at launch and is not changed afterwards, so the mechanism should keep working the same way while the token trades.

How much it pays is the variable, and that is decided entirely by transfer volume.

High activity produces large distributions, and a fall in activity reduces them in direct proportion.

The token has no treasury, no development budget, and no team spending to sustain interest, so its future depends on whether the market keeps trading it.

Readers evaluating ZCAT should watch distribution totals and volume rather than looking for product announcements that the project has no mechanism to make.

## Competitors of Anonymous Cat (ZCAT)

ZCAT belongs to two overlapping categories.

The first is the broad Solana meme coin market, where tokens compete on narrative and attention rather than on technology.

The second is narrower and more useful for comparison: reward coins, which are launches that redirect a transfer tax to holders in the asset they are paired against.

Within that second category, the competition is not really between mascots.

It is between which paired asset a trader wants to be paid in.

### Similar Tokens to Anonymous Cat

- **STONK** — the launchpad's own token, which captures platform revenue rather than paying a transfer tax to holders.
- **Equity-paired reward coins** — launches quoted against tokenized stocks or pre-IPO shares, which pay holders in that equity exposure instead of in a cryptocurrency.
- **Conventional cat-themed Solana meme coins** — tokens sharing the same visual category but with no reward mechanism attached.

## How ZCAT Differs From a Standard Solana Meme Coin

A standard Solana meme coin is quoted against SOL or a stablecoin and carries no transfer tax.

Holding one produces nothing, and the only way to realise a gain is to sell at a higher price than you paid.

ZCAT changes both variables.

It is quoted against ZEC, and it charges 3% on every transfer, which is then paid back to holders in ZEC.

The trade-off is symmetrical and worth stating clearly.

A holder gains a second income stream, but every movement of the token costs 3%, which raises the cost of entering, exiting, and moving the position between wallets or platforms.

Whether that trade-off is favourable depends on how long the position is held and how much volume the token generates while it is held.
