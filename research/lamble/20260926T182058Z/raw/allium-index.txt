- [Transaction finality](https://docs.allium.so/guides/finality.md): What it means for a transaction to be irreversible, and how many confirmations Allium waits before treating a block as settled on each chain.
- [Reorgs](https://docs.allium.so/guides/reorgs.md): Why blockchains discard blocks, what that does to data you have already read, and how Allium detects and corrects reorged records.

### Tokens & Wallets

- [Token standards](https://docs.allium.so/guides/token-standards.md): ERC-20, ERC-721, ERC-1155, SPL, Token-2022 and the rest — what each standard specifies and why the differences make blockchain data hard.
- [Hot vs cold wallets](https://docs.allium.so/guides/wallets.md): Custody models explained, and what each one looks like in onchain data.

### Trading & DeFi

- [NFT Wash-Trading Flags Schema Update](https://docs.allium.so/changelog/deprecated-nft-wt-v1.md): Schema update for polygon.nfts.trades_with_wash_trading_flags and solana.nfts.trades_with_wash_trading_flags. The _v2 tables let you preview and test the new schema before the cutover on June 19, 2026.
- [Sui JSON-RPC → gRPC Migration](https://docs.allium.so/changelog/migration-sui-grpc.md): Completed 24 September 2026. Sui raw datasets are now gRPC-sourced, replacing JSON-RPC ahead of Sui's JSON-RPC deprecation. Most schemas are unchanged; a small number of fields changed shape.
